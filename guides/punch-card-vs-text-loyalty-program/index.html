Paper Punch Card vs. Text Message Loyalty Program

By Kevin Graham · Updated September 2026

Short answer: A paper punch card is cheap and simple, but you never learn who your customers are and you can't reach them after they leave. A text message loyalty program costs more, but it captures each customer's number with permission, rewards visits automatically, and lets you bring customers back with offers and reminders.

When a paper punch card is enough

If you're just starting out, have very few repeat customers, or can't commit a small monthly budget, a punch card is better than nothing. It still rewards repeat visits.

When a text program pays for itself

If you have a steady flow of customers who could visit more often, the ability to reach them is what makes the difference. A punch card only works when the customer is already standing at your counter. A text program works when they're at home deciding where to eat, shop, or book.

A simple test: estimate how many customers you see in a month and your average ticket, then see what one extra visit is worth. Compare that with what a text program costs.

Switching from punch cards

You don't have to cut off existing punch card holders. Most businesses honor existing cards and invite those customers to join the text program, often with a bonus visit credited for switching.

Frequently asked questions

Do customers still like punch cards?

Many do, because they're simple. A digital punch card keeps that same visible progress toward a reward, without a card to carry or lose.

Is a text loyalty program hard for older customers?

Usually not. Joining takes a phone number and a tap on a kiosk, and rewards arrive as ordinary text messages. No app is required.

Can I use both?

Yes, especially during a switch. Over time most businesses move fully to the text program because it does everything the card does, plus follow-up.
